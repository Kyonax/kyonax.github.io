* [Unreleased] — 2026-08-20 :: Cross-browser floor signed, Lightning CSS pipeline, browser-support gate

*Summary:* Split the meta copy by audience (EN tuned for international and US conversion intent,
ES tuned for the national Colombian market with multi-city search coverage), eliminated layout
shift by shipping intrinsic image dimensions, added an SVG favicon, ratcheted HSTS on the apex,
and gave the privacy pages reciprocal hreflang. Then signed the browserslist floor (candidate B:
=defaults and fully supports css-lch-lab=), made the toolchain obey it through Lightning CSS and
a browserslist-derived esbuild target, and added a five-check =check-browser-support= gate, each
check proven by reintroducing its bug. Main CSS 11.46 to 11.34 KB gzip, main JS 178.02 to 177.94.
Then (2026-09-11, blog overhaul Lanes 1 and 2) replaced Google Analytics and its cookie banner with
cookieless Umami Cloud, rewrote the privacy policy around it with an opt-out toggle, and retired
the table-of-contents lab, keeping design A (the ledger) as the only table of contents. Then
(Lane 3) built the validated blog landing: the Galaxy hero, a marquee of the corpus's own numbers
recounted on every build, and three animated pipeline drawings, all prerendered and monochrome;
and (Lane 4.1) put the reading time on the archive rows and the recent cards. Then (Lane 4) gave
the article a share button, a tags row that searches the archive, a support block waiting on its
vendor, h-entry microformats and rel=me, added RSS per locale and llms.txt, and fixed a sitemap
that had never listed a single article. Then (Lane 5) published a kitchen-sink reference post in
both locales, audited it at all fourteen widths (154 failures, run red first) and fixed every one
site-side: the article's layout shift went from 0.32 to 0.002 and its Lighthouse performance score
from 0.83 to 0.99. Then (2026-09-12) refined the article from the owner's phone and Firefox
report: sharing moved to a nav icon and a designed popup, tag chips stop breaking their words,
code wraps on a phone with a WRAP toggle, equations fit their column, a footnote is one target,
the viewer gained a zoom toolbar, the cover opens in it, and (engine-side) chart labels wrap.
Then (2026-09-14 and 15, blog refinement round 2) named the blog Kyonax Build in Public in both
locales with the owner's signed SEO copy and a CollectionPage and Blog graph whose author is the
landing's Person, paged the archive (ten posts a page; page N keeps the h1 with a Page N of M line
and a head of its own), turned the archive search into a filter of All Posts at =?search==
(=?q== still works), warmed every page link on hover, focus and touch, let Chromium prerender blog
links, had every prerendered blog page name its own chunks, and kept related posts and prev/next
inside their language. From the owner's review: the author's name links home, the marquee is
built at compile time and never hydrates, the article crumb reads Blog again, and the section
rail names the section a jump landed on (it named a later one on any article with short
sections). Then (Wave C) cut the blog covers at 480, 720 and 960 px in AVIF and WebP with srcset
(page 1 at 390 px went from 1.74 MB of images to 46 KB), gave the archive a Kyonax Build in Public
share card per locale, dated the sitemap's archives by their newest post and took page N out of
it, named the blog in llms.txt, and took the landing's portrait preload and hiring keywords off
blog pages.

- HSTS kept apex-only (no =includeSubDomains= / =preload=) so future Hostinger subdomains are

- *Candidate B floor over keeping the old query:* the old query declared support for six targets
  (chrome 109, UC, QQ, KaiOS, Opera Mini) that render no palette today and cannot be made whole
  by any tool (99 of 103 =color-mix()= calls take =var()= arguments; Opera Mini has no CSS custom
  properties). B gives up 1.30 coverage points and in exchange everything declared actually works.

  +2,608 B gzip under the old targets against Lightning's +563 B for the same guarded palette,

  byte-noise in both directions (es2022 saves 104 B, es2017 would cost 1,437 B).

  website id is a public identifier (like the GA measurement id was) and lives in =data.js=, not an
  env var. A self-hosted Umami is documented in =docs/analytics.md= and not built.
- *The opt-out fails closed:* when =localStorage= is unavailable the tracker is not loaded.
- *Table of contents:* design A, the ledger, is the only one; B and C were deleted in one change.

  version from the binary that actually built the posts, passed as =--engine-version=); with no

  build time and is never faked.
